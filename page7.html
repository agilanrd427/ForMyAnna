To My SleepyHead, My Roll Eyes Queen, My Champ, My Doctor, My Love,

I may not have lived your story, but somehow I feel like I’ve walked through parts of it with you. Every little detail you’ve shared, every playful word, every guarded silence—I see you, I feel you. I’m not here to rush you or to ask you to change who you are. I’m here because I truly enjoy your presence, your energy, and the way you are, even on your ‘tak kisah’ days. You don’t have to prove anything to me. Just being you is more than enough. And if, someday, you ever feel like letting someone a little closer, I’ll still be here—steady, soft, and real. You’re already special to me, and I hope you always remember that.

No rush, no preasure. Take your very own sweetest time. Anytime, if hati you terdetik yang "He's the one! Only one!", just call out my name. I'll be all yours forever.
